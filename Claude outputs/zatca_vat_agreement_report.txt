WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE OLD FILE (output/zatca_vat_agreement.json, 134 entries)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This was not merely "3 mislabeled numbers." The old file's chunking was
fundamentally broken end-to-end:

1. Mangled source text: the old file's "text" fields contain corrupted
   Arabic — words are internally scrambled/character-reordered (e.g. "تعاريف"
   rendered "فيراعت", "الأول" rendered "لولأا") throughout, consistent with a
   raw/naive text-layer extraction from the PDF that did not correctly handle
   the PDF's underlying glyph/run ordering. Page-number junk (e.g. "27 - 2",
   "4 - 27") from page footers/headers was scraped in-line as if it were part
   of the article body.

2. "article_number" is not a real article number at all — it is just a
   sequential row index (1 to 134) over raw scraped text fragments. It has no
   relationship to the document's actual printed "المادة (N)" numbering:
   - The real, single Article 1 (التعاريف — one glossary article with ~30
     defined terms) was fragmented across several old-index entries (old
     entries labeled around indices 1, 3, 4), each holding an arbitrary slice
     of the definitions text, with page-footer numbers ("4 - 27") bleeding
     into the fragment boundaries.
   - The real Article 2 (نطاق الضريبة), Article 3 (احتساب المواعيد), Article 4
     (المجموعة الضريبية), Articles 5-8 (توريد السلع / نقل السلع / توريد
     الخدمات / التوريد المفترض) etc. are likewise not aligned 1:1 with the old
     file's index numbers past the first few entries.
   - By old-index ~108-130, the real printed headings "المادة (66)" through
     "المادة (77)" appear buried inside those entries — i.e. real articles in
     the high-60s/70s range were being indexed as if they were old article
     numbers in the 108-130 range, a roughly 40-50 position drift accumulated
     from repeated over-splitting and under-splitting earlier in the
     document.
   - Old-index 134 is actually the closing/signature clause after the real
     Article 79, not a 134th article.
   In short: the old file conflates "one row per scraped text chunk" with
   "one row per legal article." Some real articles were split across 2-3 old
   rows; other stretches of scraped text merged parts of two adjacent real
   articles into a single old row; and the running index number drifts
   further from the true article number with each such split/merge, so by
   the second half of the document the old article_number bears no relation
   to the real printed article number.

3. Net effect: the true document has 79 real numbered articles (المادة (1)
   through المادة (79)), not 134. The old file's 134 "articles" were an
   artifact of chunking granularity, not a citation-safe representation of
   the law.
