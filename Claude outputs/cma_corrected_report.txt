SAUDI CAPITAL MARKET LAW (نظام السوق المالية) — CORRECTION REPORT
====================================================================
Prepared: 2026-09-14
Source PDF verified against: official CMA Law PDF (44 pages), the current/latest
version, page-by-page, using both direct visual reading of every page and an
independent Arabic OCR pass as a cross-check.
Files produced:
  - cma_corrected.json   (68 entries — the fixed, verbatim corpus)
  - cma_corrected_report.txt   (this document)


1. TRUE TOTAL ARTICLE COUNT
----------------------------
The Law contains 67 numbered articles (المادة الأولى → المادة السابعة والستون),
PLUS one extra, specially-numbered article that Saudi legislative practice
inserts between Article 20 and Article 21 without renumbering anything after
it: "المادة العشرون مكرر" (literally "Article Twenty — Repeated", i.e.
"Article 20 bis"). It has its own two paragraphs (about listing-requirement
fairness and a ban on the market distributing profits without Board approval).

    TRUE TOTAL = 67 ordinary articles + 1 "bis" article = 68 distinct,
    separately citable legal provisions.

Because "20 bis" isn't a whole number, it cannot be stored as a normal
integer article_number without colliding with either Article 20 or Article 21.
In cma_corrected.json it is stored as article_number = 20.5 (a value that
sorts correctly between 20 and 21) and its text field starts with the exact
heading "المادة العشرون مكرر" so it is unambiguous and fully verbatim. This
is the one deliberate deviation from a strict "article_number is always a
plain integer" rule, and it is flagged here so the project owner can decide
whether to keep 20.5, switch to a string ID such as "20-bis", or represent it
some other way in the database schema.


2. SEQUENCE COMPLETENESS CHECK (NEW FILE)
-------------------------------------------
cma_corrected.json contains all of: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, ..., 20, 20.5, 21, 22, ...,
67 — 68 entries in total, every number appears exactly once, nothing is
skipped, and nothing is duplicated. Every entry's text begins with its own
correct Arabic ordinal heading ("المادة الأولى", "المادة الثانية", ... ,
"المادة السابعة والستون", or "المادة العشرون مكرر"), and was re-typed
directly from the source PDF rather than copied from the old broken file.


3. WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG WITH THE OLD FILE (output/cma.json, 66 entries)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The old file was NOT simply "off by one from Article 1 onward" — that was a
reasonable first guess, but checking every single entry against the PDF shows
the real picture is messier. The old file was built from an automated text
extraction that frequently lost track of where one article ended and the next
began. The damage comes in several different flavors, concentrated mostly in
the first third of the law (roughly Articles 1–30 / Chapters 1–4):

  A) Two entire articles are missing from the old file with ZERO trace:
     - Article 1 (المادة الأولى) — the full definitions list (المملكة، الهيئة،
       المجلس، الرئيس، الشخص، السوق، التداول، ...). This text does not appear
       anywhere in the 66 old entries.
     - Article 3 (المادة الثالثة) — the exclusions list (checks, promissory
       notes, documentary credits, cash transfers, interbank instruments,
       insurance policies are NOT "securities"). Also completely absent.

  B) One extra article is missing from the old file because it was silently
     swallowed into the wrong entry instead of getting its own record:
     - "المادة العشرون مكرر" (Article 20 bis) — its two paragraphs are
       physically present in the old file, but glued onto the end of the old
       entry labelled article_number = 20, with no heading break recognized.
       A user searching or citing "Article 20 bis" specifically would find
       nothing under that name in the old file.

  C) Several other real articles were merged into the WRONG neighboring old
     entry, so the old entry's stated number does not match what's actually
     inside it. Concrete examples found by direct comparison with the PDF:
     - Old entries labelled 1 and 3 actually contain the text of real
       Articles 2 and 4 respectively (each with the wrong heading baked into
       the start of the text, exactly as first suspected).
     - Old entries labelled 5, 6 and 7 scramble the real Articles 5, 6, 7 and
       8 across the wrong boundaries: old #5 ends up holding the tail of real
       Article 5 plus only the first half (items 1–11) of real Article 6; old
       #6 holds the second half of Article 6 (items 12–22) plus ALL of real
       Article 7; old #7 holds the tail of Article 7 plus all of real
       Article 8.
     - Old entry labelled 11 holds all of real Article 11 AND all of real
       Article 12 merged together; real Article 12 has no entry of its own.
     - Old entry labelled 12 is mislabeled — its actual content is real
       Article 13.
     - Old entries labelled 24 and 27 cross-contaminated: part of real
       Article 27 (paragraphs ج–ح) ended up appended to the end of old
       entry #24, while old entry #27 only kept the first two paragraphs
       (أ, ب) of the real Article 27.

  D) A few old entries are essentially EMPTY — they contain only the article
     heading plus a stray page header/footer fragment, with no actual legal
     text at all:
     - Old entries labelled 10, 13 and 18 (nominally corresponding to real
       Articles 11, 14 and 19) have no usable body text whatsoever.

  E) From roughly Article 31 onward (Chapters 5 through 10, i.e. the sections
     on brokers/members, investment funds, disclosure, fraud & insider
     trading, tender offers, and penalties), the old file's article numbering
     is essentially correct and 1-to-1 with the real law — but the TEXT ITSELF
     is riddled with a different, pervasive problem: the underlying PDF-to-text
     extraction dropped or mis-rendered hamza characters (e.g. "الإجراءات"
     turned into "ا¶جراءات", "لأحكام" into a mangled fragment), scrambled the
     word order within lines (a right-to-left text direction bug), and
     injected stray page headers/footers ("نظام السوق المالية", page numbers,
     chapter titles) in the middle of sentences. None of this is fabricated
     new wording — the same words are there — but as delivered it was NOT
     safe to cite verbatim, because you could not tell where a genuine legal
     phrase ended and injected page furniture began.

  Because of (A)–(E), a simple "renumber everything by +1" fix would NOT have
  produced a correct file — some regions need a +1 shift, some need a 1-to-1
  mapping with no shift, some need two old entries un-merged back into three
  or four real articles, and two articles needed to be typed from scratch
  because no trace of them existed anywhere in the old file. For that reason,
  every single one of the 68 entries in cma_corrected.json was re-typed
  directly from the source PDF (cross-checked with a fresh Arabic OCR pass of
  every page) rather than salvaged or renumbered from the old file.

5. BOTTOM LINE FOR THE PROJECT OWNER
--------------------------------------
- The corrected file has all 67 articles of the current Capital Market Law
  plus the one "bis" article the old file lost track of — 68 provisions in
  total, verified complete with no gaps or duplicates.
- Every word was re-typed directly from the official PDF (with an independent
  OCR pass used only as a cross-check, never as the primary source), so the
  text is safe to cite verbatim in the legal-AI product.
- The old file's problem was broader than a one-number shift: it was missing
  two full articles, missing a third "bonus" article, had several articles
  merged into the wrong neighbor, and had a few entries that were essentially
  blank. From Article 31 onward the numbering was actually fine, but the text
  quality (dropped hamzas, scrambled word order, embedded page furniture) was
  not reliable for citation either.
- Recommendation: replace output/cma.json with cma_corrected.json outright
  rather than trying to patch the old file in place — the discrepancies are
  too irregular (some regions shift by one, some don't, some need un-merging)
  to fix with a simple script; a full re-ingestion, which is what was done
  here, was the safer path.
